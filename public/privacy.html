Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-06

What data does this app collect?

The Variant Confusion Detector reads your Ecwid catalog data (product names, options, combinations, pricing, images, and stock availability) solely to calculate variant confusion risk scores. This data is processed in your browser and is never transmitted to any third-party server or stored outside of your session.

How is data stored?

User preferences (scan limit, risk threshold, and display settings) are saved in your browser's localStorage. No data is stored on any external server, database, or cloud service.

Third-party services

This app does not share, sell, or transmit your catalog data to any third party. The only external communication is with the Ecwid REST API to read your store profile and product catalog.

Cookies

This app does not use cookies. It relies solely on browser localStorage for persisting your dashboard preferences.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app from your Ecwid store removes its access to your catalog data. To remove stored preferences, clear your browser's local storage for this domain. No residual data remains on any external server.

Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the support channel listed in the app's marketplace listing.
