Merchant Dashboard

Variant confusion detector for the Ecwid admin.

This app scans your catalog structure inside the Ecwid dashboard and highlights products whose option trees are most likely to confuse shoppers. It does not track visitors, does not require your own database, and does not depend on a custom Node.js backend.
