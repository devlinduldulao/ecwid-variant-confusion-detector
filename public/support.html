Frequently asked questions

How does the risk score work?

The app analyzes your product options, combinations, stock levels, images, and pricing to detect structural patterns that tend to confuse shoppers. Each detected pattern adds points to a product's total risk score. Higher scores indicate more potential friction.

Does this app modify my catalog?

No. The app only reads your catalog data to generate the audit report. It never creates, updates, or deletes any products, options, or combinations.

Where is my data stored?

All analysis happens in your browser. Only your dashboard preferences (scan limit, risk threshold) are saved to browser localStorage. No data is sent to any external server.

Why do I see "Preview mode"?

Preview mode loads sample data so you can explore the dashboard without a live Ecwid connection. When installed in the Ecwid admin, the app automatically connects to your real catalog.
